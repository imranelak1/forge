FORM: A centered SVG path uses Anime.js createDrawable and createMotionPath, with a 4-unit drawn line and a 32 by 22-unit code packet carrying an authored bracket glyph. A paused timeline follows scrolling through entrance, reading and exit states in both directions; chapter evidence metadata enters and exits with its explanation. Resize preserves wrapping and recalculates scroll thresholds; focus reveals a visible passage. Reduced motion restores the original unsplit text. No JavaScript retains readable text and the complete SVG.
