# Google sign-in logo

`google.png` is the unmodified gradient Google G downloaded from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png on 2026-09-22.
It is a Google brand asset, used for the requested Google sign-in feature in
accordance with https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
It was not generated or redrawn.

The Google button uses the Google Sans Medium 500 font, self-hosted in
`../fonts/google-sans-medium.ttf`. This is a Google Fonts subset covering the
English button labels, downloaded through the Google Fonts CSS API:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Google+Sans:wght@500&display=swap&text=Continue%20with%20GoogleOpening...

`../fonts/google-sans-OFL.txt` is from
